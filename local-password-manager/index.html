Privacy policy

Local Password Manager · browser extension

Last updated: 6 May 2026

Overview

Local Password Manager (“the extension”) stores your saved logins on your device inside Chrome. This policy describes what happens to data when you use the extension. This extension is distributed independently and does not require an account with the developer.

Data the developer collects

The developer does not operate a backend service for this extension and does not receive your saved passwords, master password, or vault contents. The extension does not send your vault to developer-controlled servers for storage or syncing as part of its core functionality.

Data stored locally

The extension may store the following in your browser profile:

  • An encrypted vault containing saved login records (for example site, username, and password fields you choose to save).
  • Cryptographic material needed to decrypt the vault (for example salt and ciphertext). Vault decryption requires your master password.

This data remains on your device under your Chrome profile unless you copy, sync, or export it using tools outside this extension.

Why the extension requests permissions

  • Storage: persist the encrypted vault locally in your browser.
  • Active tab & scripting: interact with the webpage you are using when you choose actions such as filling saved credentials.
  • Website access (HTTP/HTTPS): run bundled scripts on pages you visit so the extension can locate common login fields for autofill or capture flows while your vault is unlocked, subject to the extension’s design.

The extension’s packaged JavaScript runs locally. It is not used to load or execute remote code from third-party websites for the extension’s own logic.

Third-party services

The extension is not designed to send your saved credentials to third-party analytics or advertising services as part of normal operation. If you use browser features (such as profile sync) that are provided by your browser vendor, that is governed by your browser’s settings and policies, not this document.

Uninstalling

If you remove the extension, data stored by the extension in your browser storage may be deleted or left behind depending on Chrome’s behavior and your settings. If you need guaranteed deletion, follow Chrome’s documentation for removing extension data.
